## Global Constraints

- Mode `full-replacement-direct-v2`, harness `direct-v2`, result version2; immutable executionKind `native-replacement` or `disabled-proof`. Preserve v1/source-closure behavior and skills-v0.4.0.
- Raptor/Infra own all ESS/NLB/protection mutations and approvals. Never expose local fleet templates, arbitrary shell or generic payload execution.
- Native requires actual authenticated binding and full2→4→2 transition. Diagnostic requires exact constant original2 fleet and Gateway=false throughout; only its separately labelled verdict may omit transition. A diagnostic PASS never becomes replacement PASS.
- Keep FAIL > INCONCLUSIVE > PASS across malformed evidence, abort and cleanup faults. `finished` means orderly closure, not PASS. Stop/abort only owned processes within bounds; never automatically reverse fleet mutations.
- Historical terminal getter remains valid after tail advance/120s hold. Finish admission independently checks fresh current frames/live owners; never reseal or substitute the selected successor pair.
- Source/runtime admission remains closed until the complete A/B/C graph is reviewed/tested. No environment/test override. Qualification and native execution use identical frozen source/binary/image/skill pins and stable scope/Deployment identity, not the diagnostic run's mutable generation/ReplicaSet/process/runtime observations.
