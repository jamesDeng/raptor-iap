# PgCat transition-aware traffic evidence

## Intent and current evidence

The full two-node 2→4→3→2 replacement needs one uninterrupted, assessable traffic window across scale-out and both scale-in approvals. The 2026-10-25 request `0bad9b07-625e-42cb-a021-257775e08930` reached accepted 2→4, but its local provider feed stopped and inventory became stale. The attempt was cancelled and settled; it is **INCONCLUSIVE**, not a replacement PASS. The old feed reported no failing stage, so the exact cause is unproven. PR #295 adds fixed, non-secret failure categories for the next observation without changing acceptance.

The current provider feed rejects every ESS member that is not Healthy `InService` or `Protected` and every NLB backend whose status is not `Available`. The current assessor requires successful metrics for every ESS member in every frame. Alibaba documents `Pending`, `Pending:Wait`, `Removing`, and `Removing:Wait` as ESS lifecycle states, and `Adding`, `Configuring`, and `Removing` as NLB backend states. These rules can make an ordinary replacement transition unassessable even if the clients continue succeeding. Sources: [ESS member API](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/auto-scaling/developer-reference/api-ess-2014-08-28-describescalinginstances), [NLB backend API](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/slb/network-load-balancer/developer-reference/api-nlb-2022-04-30-listservergroupservers).

## Approaches

1. Keep strict stable-only snapshots and retry the paid test. This preserves code but will again stop if a tick observes a normal transition. Do not use.
2. Begin the assessed window after 2→4 converges. This loses evidence for the first mutation and violates the approved continuous-window design. Do not use.
3. **Chosen:** retain complete, explicit transition states in each provider snapshot and assess metrics for every NLB backend, regardless of its status or weight. Only a new pending ESS node **absent from NLB** may lack PgCat metrics. Any uncertainty, gap, or SQL failure retains its existing adverse verdict.

## Snapshot and publication contract

- Add `schemaVersion: 2` to provider inventory and retained frames. Version 2 carries exactly `schemaVersion`, `feedSession`, `sequence`, `observedAt`, `desired`, sorted `members`, sorted `memberStates`, sorted `backendStates`, and sorted `healthyRegistered`. `feedSession` is one non-secret random UUID for the local feed process; `sequence` increases on each successful publication. The assessor requires one session and a monotonic sequence throughout the window, detecting a restarted feed. Each `memberStates` row contains `id`, `lifecycle`, `health`, and `addressKnown`; each `backendStates` row contains `id`, `status`, `weight`, and `listenerHealth`. No version means historical version 1 and retains its original assessment; do not reinterpret an old INCONCLUSIVE archive as PASS. Never retain IP addresses or raw provider payloads.
- Every fresh snapshot records group desired capacity, the complete paginated ESS roster with each member's lifecycle and health, and the complete paginated NLB backend roster with each ID, status, weight, and listener health result. ESS lifecycle values are `InService`, `Pending`, `Pending:Wait`, `Protected`, `Standby`, `Stopped`, `Removing`, or `Removing:Wait`; health is `Healthy` or `Unhealthy`. NLB status values are `Adding`, `Available`, `Configuring`, or `Removing`; listener health is `Normal`, `Initial`, `Unhealthy`, or `Unavailable`. The healthy result is inferred only when the exact backend is absent from the complete `NonNormalServers` list under an enabled listener health check. All roster IDs are unique, scoped to the exact group/server group, and bounded by the four-node test. A foreign never-seen backend, changed pagination, unknown state, invalid listener/health response, or missing required ECS identity stops publication or makes assessment INCONCLUSIVE. Never silently omit a pending or removing member.
- Resolve private ECS addresses for all currently known ESS or NLB IDs. A missing address is allowed only for a new pending member that is absent from the NLB roster; record `addressKnown=false` for that ID. A backend or service-ready member lacking its exact address cannot be considered covered. Addresses are used only to reconcile the existing monitoring target ConfigMap and never enter worker inventory, JSONL, Git, or logs.
- Complete the resourceVersion-checked monitoring target reconciliation before publishing the matching worker inventory. Keep targets for every previously possibly serving node through the full assessed window, even after backend deregistration, so a later zero-client observation can be checked. After the window closes, reconcile the ConfigMap to the exact final ESS/NLB fleet and verify its targets; an obsolete target may not be left behind. Preserve the absolute 15-second refresh deadline and fail-closed behavior. Do not renew timestamps from cached or partially read data.
- The local process may log only allowlisted fixed diagnostic categories from PR #295. It must not print raw CLI, Workbench, kubeconfig, address, or provider response details.

## Frame assessment

- Validate the complete provider state and its timestamp in every frame. Continue to require ordered one-second client frames, at most a three-second poll gap, active Pod/process roster, monotonic counters and event coverage, final markers, and no SQL Failure, Timeout, Ambiguous, or Skipped outcomes. An observed SQL failure is FAIL; missing or uncertain evidence is INCONCLUSIVE.
- Every NLB backend creates a metrics obligation immediately, regardless of `Adding`, `Available`, `Configuring`, `Removing`, weight, or health. This avoids assuming that a transitional status proves traffic is impossible. The obligation persists after backend absence until a fresh complete raw-metrics frame shows **all** reported pools/users for that exact node at zero active/idle/waiting and a later fresh provider snapshot confirms backend absence. Any later nonzero observation restores the obligation. The provider feed keeps that old target through the assessed window. If a newly registered backend has no exporter or Prometheus scrape yet, the full window is INCONCLUSIVE; do not relabel it non-serving. If all backends are unhealthy, also mark that frame INCONCLUSIVE because NLB documents best-effort forwarding. NLB documents that established sessions can persist after an unhealthy backend or even removal, hence the persistent zero-client obligation. Sources: [NLB health checks](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/slb/network-load-balancer/user-guide/nlb-health-check-overview/), [NLB server-group draining](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/slb/network-load-balancer/user-guide/create-and-manage-a-server-group).
- Each node with an outstanding backend obligation must have a fresh successful Prometheus scrape and complete active/idle/waiting raw triplets for the expected test pool/user in every frame, including during deregistration and any pause before scale-in. Extra scraped nodes are permitted only if their ID is in the current or earlier exact ESS roster and their raw triplets are complete and fresh. Unknown or duplicate metrics IDs remain INCONCLUSIVE. The assessor itself tracks prior obligations and the all-pool/user zero observation; it never infers retirement merely from a missing metric. Only an ESS Pending node absent from NLB can be present without metrics.
- The provider's `healthyRegistered` set must match the available, positive-weight, normal-health backend IDs that are also current ESS members. A backend absent from the current ESS roster is valid only if that exact ID appeared earlier in the same assessed window and a fresh 4→3 or 3→2 desired-capacity decrease was observed; the separate final assessor still requires the matching approved action receipt. Its metrics obligation persists until the all-pool/user zero and absence rule above is met. A new pending ESS member absent from NLB is explicitly permitted without metrics. All other ESS/NLB disagreements are INCONCLUSIVE.
- At the first retained frame, every bound client process must have zero cumulative Failure, Timeout, Ambiguous, and Skipped counters, or a verified pre-window baseline and complete initial event history that covers the interval from capture start. No first-frame baseline may hide a negative outcome between capture start and the first poll.
- Operational replacement convergence remains separate: two distinct new Protected/Healthy nodes, both originals absent, NLB healthy, SQL probes, two exact human scale-in approvals, final traffic assessment, flag false, and cleanup. A model answer or point-in-time healthy fleet cannot upgrade the result.
