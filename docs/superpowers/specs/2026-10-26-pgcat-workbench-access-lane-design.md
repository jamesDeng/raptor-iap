# PgCat Workbench access lane for continuous rehearsal evidence

## Intent and observed limit

The second replacement-disabled 2→3→2 rehearsal on 2026-10-26 is INCONCLUSIVE. Its provider feed stopped after sequence 19 while an attached Workbench diagnostic overlapped it. The exact feed error category was not retained. An earlier controlled attached 24-second Workbench call also stopped a feed at `inventory-write`; a detached 24-second worker job allowed a separate 75-second feed to finish. These observations support a transport-contention risk, not proof of the exact second failure. The fleet was recovered to the two protected originals; neither rehearsal nor full replacement has passed.

The feed has a hard 15-second publication interval. Current controller and pending-helper Workbench calls permit 6–25 and 12 seconds respectively, so operator discipline alone cannot establish a safe live schedule.

## Approaches

1. Continue with manual timing and attached status calls. Reject: one accidental diagnostic can occupy the transport for an entire feed interval.
2. **Selected:** use one Mac-side advisory lock around every Workbench CLI process, a same-session fresh-receipt lease for external calls, and detached worker jobs for long checks. This is a local control change; it adds no workload, cloud role or credential path. It still requires a live no-scale timing proof because a short lock cannot make a slow remote command fast.
3. Move provider collection and orchestration into the cluster. Defer: it adds a workload and provider credential surface beyond this bounded rehearsal repair.

## Runtime contract

- One owner-only local lock file serializes every provider-feed, rehearsal-controller and pending-helper Workbench `exec` process **while the feed runs**. The lock is held until that CLI process exits. A contender's bounded lock wait cannot silently extend the feed's 15-second absolute deadline. Pass a cross-process deadline using the host-wide `CLOCK_MONOTONIC`; this Mac's Python 3.9 `time.monotonic()` value is process-relative and cannot be handed unchanged to another process. The existing final-receipt upload happens only after verified feed exit and is outside this lane. Any observed unwrapped Workbench call during the feed invalidates the rehearsal; the lock cannot detect other independent tools or users.
- An external controller/helper Workbench call is allowed only once per successful same-session publication sequence. Immediately before launch, the mode-0600 receipt must be under one second old locally and its `observedAt` under seven seconds old. The CLI must exit within at most five seconds and before `observedAt + 12 seconds`; otherwise the run becomes INCONCLUSIVE. A timeout, uncertain remote session or unchanged next publication is never retried under the same intent. Require a strictly newer successful receipt before another external call or mutation.
- Every worker phase that can exceed the lease runs as a worker-local detached one-use job: baseline, pre-scale, pending-readiness and zero-gate. Collector launch and every sealed-status read also consume a short lease while the feed runs. The worker job has a private intent/identity/result receipt and does not hold the Mac-side Workbench session. Do not infer success from the launch acknowledgement alone.
- Final target reconciliation is one resourceVersion-bound worker action with an exact sealed result in the same short leased Workbench call. After a later successful provider publication, stop and verify the feed before the existing post-stop validation and finish. The active runbook forbids extra Workbench status polling after final reconciliation while the feed is running. If a comparable read-only timing surrogate cannot fit the lease, redesign closure; even if it fits, the real combined call remains an action-time five-second gate in the paid rehearsal. Do not split it into a detached action followed by live-feed status polling.
- The local lock and lease are operational controls, not traffic acceptance evidence. Retain one continuous provider session and collector; preserve strict poll, freshness, metric, roster and SQL rules. Keep `replacementEnabled=false` for rehearsal. An interrupted feed or collector is INCONCLUSIVE; preserve only sanitized partial evidence and perform a separately reviewed exact recovery of any changed fleet state.
- The collector supervisor must bind the exact child PID, boot/start identity and command, reset inherited SIGINT in that child, accept one stop intent, and record a sealed exit result. The still-parent supervisor alone signals its unreaped child, after receiving one bounded identity/nonce packet through a mode-0600 private FIFO under the mode-0700 run directory. It must close admission before reaping natural completion; `Popen.poll()` and `waitpid(WNOHANG)` reap and cannot be used while the FIFO still accepts a cancel. A non-reaping `waitid(..., WNOWAIT)` check or equivalent is required. An uncertain FIFO, parent or acknowledgement cannot be retried or converted into PASS. The capture child receives only fixed `PATH`, `HOME`, `LANG`, `NO_PROXY` and exact `KUBECONFIG`, never ambient cloud token/proxy/credential variables. Preserve partial evidence and label it INCONCLUSIVE if clean closure is unproved.
- The feed supervisor retains only an allowlisted, credential-free exit category and code in a mode-0600 local receipt. Raw stderr and Workbench output never enter the handoff or evidence archive. This explains a future interruption without turning it into a successful publication.
