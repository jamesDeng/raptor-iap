# PgCat Mac-lane no-scale closure

## Intent and observed defect

The replacement-disabled no-scale proof must show that the Mac lane owns its worker calls, receives later provider publications, retains a complete finite traffic capture, and closes its exact child processes. It is an operational timing proof before the separately assessed paid 2→3→2 rehearsal. It cannot prove a replacement transition or substitute for rehearsal PASS.

The current runbook fixes a 75-second feed at 15-second publication intervals, with baseline start/status and collector launch/status each followed by a later publication. Even one baseline status completion consumes approximately four successive intervals. The historical collector control is untracked, embeds retired paths and starts a 5,400-second capture. A 60-second capture launched around the third call exits after the 75-second feed; there is no later publication for a completed-exit status. The ordinary transition assessor also requires a transition and client final markers that this no-scale mode deliberately lacks. Thus the current path cannot honestly produce its required `noScaleProof.status=PASS` receipt.

## Approaches

- Keep 75 seconds and shorten the feed interval or weaken the successor requirement. This reduces slack while changing the publication contract and may still miss collector exit. Reject.
- Keep 75 seconds and accept `ALIVE` or an ordinary assessor `INCONCLUSIVE` as success. This would make an unfinished capture look complete. Reject.
- Use a fixed 180-second maximum no-scale feed at the unchanged 15-second cadence, one finite 65-second collector, an exact `ALIVE` then completed-exit record and a named lane-timing verdict. Choose this bounded correction. It adds at most about 105 seconds of read-only provider polling and no resource or replacement mutation.

The user's standing instruction is to keep working and avoid repeated approval pauses. This document makes that local implementation choice reviewable; live proof still requires source review and preflight. The earlier instruction not to silently extend the 75-second cap is honored by changing the runbook and tests explicitly before any live use.

## Design

The no-scale controller admits only fixed baseline, collector and lane-verdict actions in immutable `no-scale` mode. It starts one fresh feed with a **maximum** 180-second loop at the unchanged 15-second interval, and requires a fixed 120-second startup plus 180-second loop plus 60-second cleanup reserve before 22:30 Shanghai; admission therefore closes at 22:24. Every action still consumes a fresh same-session successful receipt observed after the call, within existing five-second action and 12-second receipt leases. The no-scale controller never admits Pending, NLB, ESS, Gateway, client finish, or ConfigMap mutation. Baseline status may poll only within the fixed feed window; no automatic extension or second feed is allowed. The controller passes its exact validated target as a fixed address-free feed argument; the feed hashes it and requires equality with the supervisor's sealed `run-context.json.targetSha256` before polling. A no-scale feed validator checks those exact two protected originals and healthy NLB backends on every publication; any changed fleet stops the feed and prevents PASS.

Promote a fresh, source-pinned worker collector control with bindings from the private run root. It launches one 65-second `worker_capture.py` without a finish boundary, retains its exact collector identity and completion file, and exposes fixed `ALIVE` and `EXITED` status. `EXITED` is accepted only when the exact previously observed child has code zero, a sealed capture completion says completed true/cancelled false, and its exit category is completed. Unknown identity, missing final file, nonzero exit, timeout or an extra launch is INCONCLUSIVE. Controller reads `EXITED` through the same supervisor-owned Workbench lane and requires a later successful provider publication.

Extend the existing sanitized schema-2 frame only with validated, bounded non-secret fields needed for this proof: each Pod's name and container ID, exact Ready condition, and the Deployment UID, generation, observed generation and ready/desired replica counts. Retain these on every poll; historical frames without them remain readable for their earlier purpose but cannot satisfy lane-timing PASS. The exact two Pod UID/container ID/process ID tuples and one Deployment UID/generation remain identical throughout the no-scale capture, with two Ready replicas. A partial or missing read is INCONCLUSIVE. Do not retain Pod IPs, secret-bearing fields or a Kubernetes object dump.

A separate **worker-local, source-pinned** `lane-timing` assessor verifies the sealed completion file and every immutable segment hash. It requires at least 60 seconds of authenticated first-to-last frame coverage from the planned 65-second capture at one-second target cadence, at-most-three-second poll gaps, exact two original protected/Healthy ESS members and Available/Normal NLB backends of weight 100 in every frame, one feed session with no skipped **distinct** sequence (repeated sequence is expected between 15-second feed ticks) and provider age under 30 seconds, fresh complete per-node `up=1` and all active/idle/waiting raw metrics (including `test/poc_app`) with source age at most 45 seconds, and the exact stable two Ready clients/Deployment identities above. Both client SQL streams must advance, with zero Failure/Timeout/Ambiguous/Skipped and no adverse observed event. An authenticated `connection_failure` event is FAIL; unexplained within-window disconnect/reconnect without a recorded SQL failure is INCONCLUSIVE. A missing or malformed interval, incomplete source, changed identity, stale observation or uncertain collector exit is INCONCLUSIVE; any authenticated observed SQL failure is FAIL even if another interval is incomplete.

The assessor runs through one fixed supervisor-owned Workbench action after the `EXITED` successor, with exact staged source hashes checked before execution. It seals a private one-use verdict bound to the current run/feed session, target/source manifest, exact collector identity and capture completion/segment digests. The sanitized response contains only status, category and the verdict SHA-256. The Mac supervisor journals that response, and the controller verifies it against the same action result. Terminal text or a caller-created PASS file cannot replace this worker verdict. The ordinary transition assessor remains INCONCLUSIVE because no replacement or client finalization happened.

After the lane-verdict action and its later successful provider publication, the controller writes one owner-only, fsynced **graceful stop intent** naming the active run, feed session, minimum sequence and sealed verdict hash. The feed checks it after each successful publication and before the next provider read; it may publish at most one more exact-two observation if the intent arrives during a tick, then exits zero. It never fabricates a fresh inventory timestamp. A missing, changed or unacknowledged stop intent is INCONCLUSIVE; the fixed 180-second natural cap remains a fallback, not a PASS path. This keeps the uncaptured tail to at most one publication interval and leaves the final receipt fresh for bounded finalization. The controller retains the exact feed `Popen` handle, verifies actual exit and final status/session/sequence, and refuses if its final receipt is 30 seconds old or more.

The final Mac no-scale assessment is a deterministic combination of the sealed worker verdict, exact final provider receipt, feed stop acknowledgement and exit receipt; its `endSequence` is the **actual** final sequence and its evidence hash covers all those inputs. The controller passes only that assessment to `finish_no_scale`, and the supervisor checks its journaled worker-verdict digest before committing `finished`. The client must resolve a race where the supervisor exits after durably committing `finished` but before the caller's liveness check: re-read the exact sealed final journal state/assessment on that path, never infer success from process exit or an older active snapshot. PASS requires the worker verdict PASS, every final feed check and exact process/credential cleanup; a `finished` journal state never overrides FAIL/INCONCLUSIVE. The no-scale proof record also seals the run date, stable target/source-bundle/client-identity hashes and its original private binding-file hash for provenance. The later paid run uses **fresh** private paths and therefore a different binding-file hash; its preflight separately verifies its own new binding and compares the stable target/source/client hashes with the authenticated no-scale proof. The paid preflight's `noScaleProof` names the previous absolute private proof path and evidence hash; it must open the mode-0600, no-symlink proof under a mode-0700 directory and refuse a stale, changed or unrelated PASS hash. Final cleanup verifies exact local feed/worker collector process exit and removes only the named temporary credential copies and active controls. Retain the authenticated capture, verdict and exit evidence. A dropped Workbench call never proves remote cancellation. Keep `replacementEnabled=false` throughout.

## Verification and rollout

Use fake processes and fixtures to prove the 180-second maximum, deterministic 22:24 admission cutoff, action sequencing, one collector launch, 65-second completion with at least 60 seconds of authenticated coverage, later publications, one-use graceful stop, no-scale allowlist, missing/changed identity and timeout refusal. Assess authenticated good and adverse captures, including observed SQL failure precedence, missing polls, stale metrics, a client restart, changed Deployment, changed roster and a malformed worker verdict. Test a stop intent arriving during a feed tick and an expired final receipt; neither can create PASS. Run normal/optimized Python suites and Python 3.6 syntax checks for worker code. Independently review source and exact staged bytes, then perform one live disabled no-scale proof without ESS/NLB/Gateway mutation. Only its retained lane-timing PASS and exact cleanup can enter the paid preflight receipt. Paid rehearsal and full replacement remain separate gates.
