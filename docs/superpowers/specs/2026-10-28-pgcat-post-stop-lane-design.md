# PgCat fixed post-stop lane admission

## Intent, scope and choice

Connect the reviewed exact finish worker to one actual journal-bound post-stop action. Normal live-feed calls keep their freshness/successor rules. Public paid release remains no-scale-only. No provider/clock-anchor change, full verdict/abort/restoration/cleanup/native observer or runtime proof is supplied by this unit.

The user authorized continuous implementation/test/review and opted out of repeated approval questions. Controller choice: one fixed `rehearsal-finish` action and `post-stop` wrapper role. A generic command/role bypass would widen authority; ordinary publication-sequence admission cannot authorize this frozen receipt because its provider has actually exited. Use actual stop provenance and one semantic claim instead.
